About Us

Small shop.
Every order made by hand.

Ink'd Custom Creations is a family-run print and engraving shop. Every wallet, tumbler, shirt, and banner that goes out the door is made to order - no big-box templates, no minimums.

We work with individuals, teams, schools, and small businesses who need something personal done right: a laser-engraved gift for someone specific, or a hundred matching hoodies for opening day. If you can picture it, we can probably put ink, thread, or a laser to it.
